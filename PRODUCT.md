# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating a freelance web developer/designer: small and
medium business owners and founders, primarily in the Dominican Republic
(Spanish-first, Dominican market is a first-class audience, not a translation
afterthought) and secondarily international/English-speaking. They land from
search or referral, often on a Spanish geo landing page, deciding in the first
screen whether this person can be trusted to build their site. Their job: judge
credibility fast, understand what's offered, and reach out (or pay) with low
friction.

## Product Purpose

DR Web Studio is James Karnes's freelance portfolio and client-facing site. It
exists to win web projects: demonstrate craft, communicate the service range
(custom sites, e-commerce, landing pages, CMS, maintenance, SEO), and convert
visitors into leads (contact form, project-planner brief) and payments (Stripe
checkout). Success = a visitor believes the work is high quality and starts a
conversation or a payment. Because the seller is a web designer/developer, the
site itself is the single strongest proof of capability.

## Brand Personality

Bold and modern, with confidence carried through restraint rather than volume.
Voice: direct, design-forward, human; speaks plainly about what gets built and
why it matters, never in marketing buzzwords. Three words: confident,
contemporary, credible. The site should feel like it was made by someone with
genuine taste, distinct from the freelance-web-dev category default.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS/Squarespace/Wix-template look: identical icon+title+text card
  grids, gradient CTAs as the only color move, a tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow
  above every section.
- Corporate and sterile: cold, faceless big-agency feel, stock photography,
  zero personality.
- Loud and flashy: over-animation, neon, gimmicky effects that distract from
  the work itself.
- Cluttered and busy: too much on screen at once, weak hierarchy, every element
  competing for attention.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the proof.** Practice what you preach: this portfolio must
   itself demonstrate the craft James sells. Show capability, don't just
   describe it.
2. **Bold through restraint.** Be distinctive and modern without being loud or
   cluttered. Commit to a point of view; earn attention with hierarchy and
   typography, not noise.
3. **Escape the category reflex.** Actively refuse the freelance-web-dev /
   SaaS-template defaults (orange-gradient CTAs everywhere, repeated card grids,
   per-section eyebrows). If it looks like every other freelance site, rework it.
4. **Bilingual parity is first-class.** Spanish (Dominican market) is a primary
   audience, not a localized afterthought. Every layout, headline, and label
   must hold up in both en and es (line length, wrapping, hreflang).
5. **Every section earns conversion.** The funnel (contact, project-planner,
   Stripe) is the point; each section should move a visitor forward or justify
   its space, without feeling salesy or templated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: body text >=4.5:1 contrast, large text >=3:1, visible
focus states, full keyboard navigation, and a `prefers-reduced-motion`
alternative for every animation (the site uses Motion scroll-reveals/hovers
today). Explicit bilingual care: verify contrast, line length, label wrapping,
and CTA fit in both English and Spanish, since es copy runs longer.
